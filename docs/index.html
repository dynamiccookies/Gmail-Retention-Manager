Keep email only as long as you need it.

Simple policies

Your labels define the retention period.

Apply a label yourself or let an ordinary Gmail filter apply it automatically.

1

Label

Use a label such as Retention/30d on conversations that should be kept for 30 days.

2

Schedule

Choose how often Retention Manager checks labeled conversations for expiration.

3

Review

Receive a deletion summary with direct links to the conversations moved to Gmail Trash.

Built for control

Automation without surrendering visibility.

Runs in your account

The manually installed app executes inside your Google Apps Script environment.

Only labeled conversations

No default retention period is imposed. You decide which conversations receive retention labels.

Recoverable deletion

Expired active messages are moved to Gmail Trash, not immediately deleted forever.

Operational status

The Gmail sidebar shows the schedule, last run, next run, and current runtime state.

Detailed administration

Manage labels, notifications, schedules, backups, diagnostics, and simple filter cleanup.

Clear reporting

Summaries report what moved and provide direct Gmail links when review is needed.
